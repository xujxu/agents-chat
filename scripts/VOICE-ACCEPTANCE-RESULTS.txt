4. Remaining gates and recommended direction
-------------------------------------------
The completed experiment does not support shipping either candidate unchanged.
To continue without raising limits, evaluate an explicitly approved bounded
segmentation/VAD candidate with the corrected memory monitor.
Measure boundary omissions/repetitions on the same natural audio; never join
source clips to pretend they were a natural long input.

Do not infer pure resampling accuracy loss from the current paired failure-
inclusive totals: changing success sets confounds that comparison. The reviewed
report separately compares paired successful cases in paired-success.json while
retaining all failures as primary delivery evidence. This reanalysis uses the
same saved inferences, without rerunning models or changing configurations.

Actual microphone/AEC, other devices, WebKit corpus accuracy, broader natural
mixed speech and speaker/domain generalization remain unproven. Product quality
and latency acceptance thresholds still required agreement at this checkpoint. No passed workflow
or small corpus score is permission to deploy or increase service/CLI limits.
